# Authored car and material assets

`proof-concept.glb` and `hero-concept.glb`: **Car Concept**, model and textures by Eric Chadwick,
Darmstadt Graphics Group GmbH, © 2024. Creative Commons Attribution 4.0
International: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Source, detailed authoring notes and legal credits:
https://github.com/KhronosGroup/glTF-Sample-Assets/tree/main/Models/CarConcept

Downloaded from the official Khronos glTF Sample Assets repository on 2026-10-01.
Changes: normalized scale/orientation, paint recolor, tire roughness, lighting
and shadow setup. The gameplay variant `hero-concept.glb` additionally uses
Draco geometry compression; rendering batches static parts by material.
Source geometry and texture maps are retained.
Khronos and 3D Commerce logos remain part of the source asset; no affiliation
or endorsement is implied. This is a temporary quality benchmark, not a final
hero-car design.

`venice_sunset_1k.hdr` and `venice_sunset_2k.hdr`:
Venice Sunset, Poly Haven, CC0.
https://polyhaven.com/a/venice_sunset
https://polyhaven.com/license

Road007 color, normal and roughness maps: ambientCG, CC0.
https://ambientcg.com/view?id=Road007

The isolated material proof is accessible at `?proof`. Ordinary gameplay uses
`hero-concept.glb` for the player and traffic, with the 2K HDRI and Road007 maps.
The original controls remain available. Earlier proof before/after captures
compare assets in a separate stage; gameplay captures show the integrated scene.
